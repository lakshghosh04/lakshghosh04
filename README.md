### ◈ The Perspective

I like starting with the business question, not the tool.

What is changing? Why does it matter? And what can the data help us do about it?

That curiosity shapes my interests in **Business Analytics, Business Intelligence and Technology Consulting**, where analysis becomes most valuable when people can use it.
